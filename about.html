ABOUT THE PROJECT

BuccalCare AI

Early Buccal Cancer Screening System · Final-Year Academic Prototype

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Making screening concepts accessible

Buccal/oral lesions can sometimes be difficult to identify early, and access to specialists may be limited. This project demonstrates an accessible image-based workflow for academic exploration.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

Computer vision for risk-oriented screening

An AI-assisted interface demonstrates how oral photographs could be processed and categorized according to visual risk patterns, while encouraging professional follow-up.

PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Educational prototype boundaries

  • Images remain within the browser workflow in this frontend demo.
  • History is stored locally in the browser.
  • The demo algorithm is not clinically validated.
  • Professional examination and biopsy/histopathology may be required for diagnosis.
  • Do not upload personally identifiable medical information.
